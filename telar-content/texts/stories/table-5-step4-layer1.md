In the center of Pantitlan is the drain-like sort of whirlpool, where people give their offerings.  Surrounding this drain is a set of four stairs, in the shape of a square that ascends to create a cistern.  Surrounding the cistern then is a ring that signifies earth that has risin above the water level, and on this land is where the banners were to be placed.  The structure actually makes a sort of pyramid structure, that would only be visible during times of low water levels, and served as a sort of alarm to signify the arrival of the time for ritual.  Overall, the entire depiction represents a ritual of renewal that ensures the continuation of not just steady watter but of their universe.
