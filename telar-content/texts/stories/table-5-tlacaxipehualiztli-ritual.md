---
title: "Tlacaxipehualiztli Ritual"
---

The Tlacaxipehualiztil was both a festivity and a ritual that was done by the Nahua people, honoring the god Xipe Totec or in english known as Flayed God. Where they would choose a victim for the sacrific, and to the Nahua people this was a very crucial step in their rituals and  everyday life. As you can see in the image, there about four people, each one grabing the man either from his legs or arms and in the middle a man grabing him from his limbs. Once they kill the victim they take out all the organs and bones from the body, which is what we call skinning and after that is done, someone then puts on the skin of the victim. That person then is representing the god Xipe Totec and these people were called ixiptla, meaning impersonater because the person is impersonating the god.
